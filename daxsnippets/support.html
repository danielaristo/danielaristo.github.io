Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to use the app?

No. Once installed, DAX Snippets works fully offline — every snippet and function is bundled with the app.

Which categories are free?

Time Intelligence and Aggregations & Ratios (Snippets), plus Aggregation and Filter & Context (Functions) are free to use. A single Premium Unlock purchase opens every remaining category.

I bought Premium Unlock but switched phones — do I have to pay again?

No. Open the paywall screen on any locked category and tap “Restore purchase.” Apple restores it to your account at no extra cost.

Is this app affiliated with Microsoft?

No. DAX Snippets is an independent reference tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft. “DAX” and “Power BI” are trademarks of Microsoft Corporation.

Does the app collect or upload my data?

No, never. Everything runs on your device. See the full details in the privacy policy.
