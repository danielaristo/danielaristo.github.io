Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

In one sentence: DAX Snippets does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything runs on your device, and the app works fully offline.

§1

What information we collect

None. DAX Snippets doesn't require an account, doesn't include analytics tools or third-party trackers, and doesn't send any data to a server we operate. Every screen — the Snippets library and the Functions reference — runs entirely on your device, even without an internet connection.

§2

Your purchase state

Whether you've unlocked the Pro categories is checked directly against Apple's App Store record for your account (via StoreKit) — it isn't stored on any server we run, and we never see which formulas or functions you view or copy.

§3

Purchases

DAX Snippets is free to download, with one optional one-time in-app purchase (Premium Unlock) that unlocks every remaining Snippets and Functions category. There are no subscriptions. Apple processes the purchase; DAX Snippets never sees, receives, or stores your payment information.

See Apple's privacy policy for how it handles App Store purchases.

§4

Device permissions

DAX Snippets does not request access to your camera, photo library, location, contacts, or any other device permission. Copying a formula uses the system clipboard, which iOS already grants every app by default.

§5

Children's privacy

DAX Snippets doesn't collect information from any user, regardless of age. Because no data is collected, the app naturally complies with children's privacy regulations such as COPPA.

§6

Trademarks

DAX Snippets is an independent reference tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft. “DAX” and “Power BI” are trademarks of Microsoft Corporation.

§7

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change too. We recommend checking back occasionally.
