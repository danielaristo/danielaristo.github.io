DP-600 Mastery Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

In one sentence: DP-600 Mastery does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your study progress stays on your device, and the app works fully offline.

§1 – What information we collect

No data is gathered. The application requires no registration, contains no tracking mechanisms, and sends nothing to external servers. All functionality — practice questions, mock exam, and Weak Spots review — operates locally without internet dependency.

§2 – Your study progress

Answered questions, accuracy metrics, spaced-repetition schedules, mock exam records, and optional exam dates remain exclusively on your device. No uploading occurs. Switching devices or uninstalling results in data loss, as no cloud synchronization exists.

§3 – Purchases

The app is sold as a one-time download. The App Store purchase grants full access, including the Mock Exam and Weak Spots features. No subscriptions or in-app purchases exist. Apple handles all payment processing; the app developer never accesses payment details.

See Apple's privacy policy for App Store purchase handling.

§4 – Device permissions

Camera, photos, location, contacts, and other sensitive permissions are not requested. Local storage for progress data uses iOS default permissions automatically.

§5 – Children's privacy

No user information is collected at any age. The app inherently complies with regulations like COPPA due to its zero-collection approach.

§6 – Trademarks

This is an independent study tool with no Microsoft affiliation. "DP-600" and "Microsoft Fabric" remain Microsoft trademarks.

§7 – Changes to this policy

Policy modifications will be reflected in the "Last updated" date. Users should review periodically.
