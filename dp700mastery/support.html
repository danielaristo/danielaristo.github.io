Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to use the app?

No. Once installed, DP-700 Mastery works fully offline — practice questions, the Mock Exam, and Weak Spots review all run locally on your device.

Do I lose my progress if I delete the app or switch phones?

Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, with no account or cloud backup. There's nothing to restore on a new device.

Is this app affiliated with Microsoft?

No. DP-700 Mastery is an independent study aid, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft. Its question bank is built from Microsoft's official, publicly published Skills Measured outline for the exam, plus original scenario questions written for this app. "DP-700" and "Microsoft Fabric" are trademarks of Microsoft Corporation.

How is the Mock Exam scored?

The Mock Exam samples questions proportional to the real exam's domain weights and scores your result on a scale of 1000, with 700+ counting as a pass — matching how Microsoft reports real DP-700 results. It's an estimate based on equal weight per question; Microsoft's actual scoring model isn't published.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Reset all progress." This clears every question's history and your Mock Exam results, and can't be undone.

Does the app collect or upload my data?

No, never. Everything runs on your device. See the full details in the privacy policy.
